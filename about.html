About Us

Baro Energy Trading

Baro Energy Trading is a company established in November 23, 2011 EC and registered under the Ethiopian Commercial Registration and Business License Proc No. 980/2016. The major destinations are Gambela, North-West Ethiopia, Semera, Addis Ababa, Bure, and Mizan-Tepi. The company’s head office is located in Addis Ababa.

The company has built a strong reputation in the fuel, oil, and lubricants supply industry in Ethiopia. Adopting innovative technologies to improve logistics and deliver quality services is central to our approach.

Baro Energy Trading

Baro Energy Trading (est. December 2, 2018 GC) operates across major commercial areas of Ethiopia and offers a broad portfolio including fuel distribution, agricultural commodity export, and machinery supply.

Our operations include production, procurement, and distribution activities with focus on sustainability, quality, and digital transformation.

Fueling Ethiopia's Future, Today

Headquartered in Addis Ababa, our enterprise spans:

  • Fuel & petroleum products supply (600,000-liter depot capacity)
  • Agri-commodity production & export
  • Import of industrial inputs, machinery & lubricants
  • Contract farming with 2,000+ farmers
  • Logistics and transport services

We’re committed to quality, sustainability, and digital transformation.
